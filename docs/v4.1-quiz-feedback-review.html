② Current game logic (v4.0, unchanged this round)

Question generation

  • Each question picks a random clock time: hour 1–12, minute drawn from the "allowed set".
  • The dial shows that time statically; below it, 3 choices: 1 correct + 2 wrong.
  • Distractor strategy:
    • ① Off by one hour (the most common kid mistake, e.g. reading 3:00 as 2:00 or 4:00).
    • ② Same hour, different minute (only enabled once the difficulty allows multiple minute values).

Difficulty ladder (auto-promotes by correct streak)

Lv 1

On the hour

Streak 0–2

minute ∈ {00}

e.g. 3:00 / 7:00

Lv 2

Hour + half-hour

Streak 3–5

minute ∈ {00, 30}

e.g. 3:00 / 7:30

Lv 3

Hour + quarters

Streak ≥ 6

minute ∈ {00, 15, 30, 45}

e.g. 3:15 / 9:45

⚠️ A wrong answer resets the streak to zero and drops back to "on the hour", protecting the child's confidence.

Answer flow

  1. Question appears; wait for the kid to tap a choice.
  2. On tap, "reveal" immediately: the chosen button scales to 1.05× and bounces; correct turns green, wrong turns red.
  3. Correct: show the green ✓ banner, auto-advance to the next question after 1.4s.
  4. Wrong: show the blue "Next question" FAB and let the kid tap it themselves (giving them a beat to recover).
  5. The top speaker button replays the question on demand (using the v3.7 child-voice pack, following the current language / 12-24h / voice settings).
